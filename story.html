a case study

Friendships don't end.
They fade.

No fight, no falling out — just a text that never got sent, then another, then a year. Ember is the small app I built to catch that fade before it finishes.

the problem

You don't lose people in an argument.
You lose them in silence.

Adult life is a machine for quietly erasing friendships. Everyone's busy, everyone means well, and nobody decides to drift apart on purpose — it just happens between calendar invites. By the time you notice, the friendship isn't broken. It's just cold.

📅

Busy ≠ don't care

The people who matter most get the least deliberate attention, because urgent always beats important.

🙈

No warning system

Your phone reminds you about everything except the friendship that's been quietly cooling for months.

😶

Guilt keeps it frozen

The longer the silence, the harder the first message feels — so it keeps getting pushed another week.

the idea

Treat friendships like embers, not switches.

A fire doesn't go out the moment you stop feeding it — it cools, gradually, and you can always throw another log on before it's ash. That's the whole model behind Ember: every friendship gets a Friend Health Score that cools the longer it's been since you last connected, and warms right back up the moment you reach out.

🔥100%Thriving
🌤️70%Steady
🌥️45%Cooling
🥶20%Fading
🧊5%Cold

how it works

Three moves, no busywork.

01

Set your circle

Add the people you don't want to lose — bestie, close friend, family, whatever cadence feels right for each one.

02

Ember watches the temperature

Every friendship's score quietly cools the longer it's been — no guilt trips, just an honest number.

03

Get nudged before it's cold

A plain-language nudge — "send one text" — shows up before the friendship needs a whole reunion to fix.

under the hood

Built small, built private, built fast.

Ember is vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework, no build step, no backend. Every friend, every check-in, every score lives only in your browser's local storage. Nothing is ever sent anywhere, which means there's nothing to leak and nothing to subscribe to.

🔐

100% local

No account, no server, no analytics. Your circle stays on your device.

⚡

Zero dependencies

No npm install, no bundler — open the file and it works.

📦

Portable data

One-tap JSON backup and CSV export whenever you want a copy.
